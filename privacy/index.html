Auremetra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Auremetra ("the app") is a calculator and workshop notebook for jewelers, published by Auremetra ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

In short: everything stays on your device. The app has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot see your data. The only time the app uses the internet is to talk to Google Play about the optional subscription (see "Subscription and purchases").

What the app stores on your device

  • Calculation history — the inputs and results of your calculations.
  • Orders and casting cards you create — client names, descriptions, notes, dates, payments, and photos you attach.
  • Settings — language, theme, units, currency, prices, custom densities and burnout cycles.
  • Subscription status — a single yes/no value: whether your subscription is active.
  • Crash log — if the app crashes, a short technical record (app version, Android version, error message) is written to a file. Only the last few crashes are kept. The file is never sent anywhere automatically.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive it, and no third party receives it from the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal data, and the app does not communicate with any server run by us.

Subscription and purchases

Some features are available with the optional Auremetra Master subscription, which is sold through Google Play. The purchase, the payment and the free trial are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your name, e-mail address, card or other payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your subscription is active and keeps the answer (active or not) on your device. For this the app uses the internet and Google Play Billing. Google handles that data under Google's own privacy policy.

You can cancel the subscription or restore a purchase in Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Apart from this, the app does not send anything over the internet.

Things you can choose to do

Some features let you move data out of the app. They run only when you tap them, and you decide where the data goes:

  • Backup, export and sharing — the backup file, Excel and PDF exports, and the "send report" and "report a translation issue" buttons open the Android share sheet. The file or text goes to the app you pick (e-mail, messenger, cloud storage), and from then on that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Printing — PDF documents can be printed through Android's print service.
  • Photos — when you attach a photo, Android's camera or photo picker is used. The app receives only the picture you choose and keeps a copy in its private storage.
  • 3D model files — when you open an STL file, the app reads it on your device. The file is not uploaded, and the 3D view runs inside the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — only to check your subscription with Google Play.
  • Google Play billing — to buy and restore the subscription.
  • Notifications — to remind you about order deadlines. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Vibration — used by those reminders.
  • Run at startup — to restore those reminders after the device restarts.
  • Biometrics / screen lock — an optional lock for the Orders section. The check is done by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint, face data or PIN.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual items inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device. Files you exported or shared yourself stay wherever you sent them.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side. Your data is in your hands, on your device. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your rights under the GDPR are therefore met by design, and you can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date at the top.
